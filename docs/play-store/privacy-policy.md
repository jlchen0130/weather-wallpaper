# 隱私權政策／Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026-11-18

## 中文

「Amigurumi Weather 天氣桌布」（以下稱本 App）依照您所在縣市的天氣與時段，自動更換手機桌布。

**我們使用的資料**

| 資料 | 用途 | 是否離開手機 |
|---|---|---|
| 位置（精確或大略） | 判斷您所在的縣市與當地天氣 | 會：經緯度或縣市名稱傳送到本 App 的桌布伺服器，用來查詢天氣與挑選桌布 |
| 縣市、天氣、日期、時段 | 挑選對應的桌布圖檔 | 會：傳送到桌布伺服器 |
| 購買紀錄 | 確認您是否已購買「解鎖全部地區」 | 由 Google Play 處理，本 App 不會取得您的付款資料 |

- 免費版在第一次成功定位後，只記住縣市名稱，之後不再讀取位置。
- 已解鎖的使用者若選擇「指定地區」，App 也不再讀取位置。
- 本 App 沒有帳號系統，不收集姓名、電子郵件、聯絡人、照片或裝置識別碼，也沒有廣告或第三方追蹤。
- 桌布伺服器架設在 Cloudflare。伺服器的請求紀錄（含上述參數）僅供除錯，依 Cloudflare 的保留期限自動刪除，不會出售或分享給第三方。
- 設定與快取桌布只存在您的手機裡，解除安裝 App 即全部刪除。

**您的選擇**

您可以隨時在系統設定中關閉本 App 的定位權限。關閉後，App 會使用預設地區的桌布。

**聯絡我們**

對本政策有任何問題，請透過 Google Play 商店頁面上的開發者聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

## English

Amigurumi Weather changes your wallpaper to match the weather and time of day where you are.

**Data we use**

- **Location (precise or approximate):** used to find your county/city and its weather. Coordinates or the county/city name are sent to the app's wallpaper server to look up weather and pick a wallpaper.
- **County/city, weather, date, time of day:** sent to the wallpaper server to pick the matching image.
- **Purchase status:** Google Play tells the app whether you bought "Unlock all regions". We never see your payment details.

The free version stores only the county/city name after the first successful location fix and stops reading location afterwards. The same applies to unlocked users who pick a region manually.

The app has no accounts, ads or third-party tracking. It does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos or device identifiers. The wallpaper server runs on Cloudflare; its request logs are used only for debugging and expire under Cloudflare's retention policy. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Settings and cached wallpapers stay on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

You can turn off location permission in system settings at any time; the app then uses a default region.

Contact: use the developer contact listed on the Google Play store page.
